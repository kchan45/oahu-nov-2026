⚓ Pearl Harbor: three ways to do itBest slot: Sat 11/14 morning, on the way back toward Makaha. Skip Wed 11/11 (Veterans Day crowds).

Option 1Free USS Arizona Memorial program

Free, with a $1 non-refundable fee per ticket on Recreation.gov. Tickets release daily at 3 PM HST for next day and 8 weeks out, and same-day walk-up tickets are no longer given out. Up to 12 per person, so one person can book all 9. Reservations are final, and bags aren’t allowed (paid storage onsite).

Do this: Fri 11/13 at 3 PM HST (5 PM Pacific), book Saturday. The 8-week window for 11/14 has likely sold out, but check for cancellations.

Book on Recreation.govNPS booking rules

Option 2Guided tour or museum combo

If tickets are gone, a guided tour is the usual backup. Separately, the Pearl Harbor Passport covers the Battleship Missouri, USS Bowfin, and Aviation Museum, but does not include the USS Arizona boat program. Allow 7–8 hours to see all sites, or pick one or two.

Compare tickets and toursPearl Harbor Historic Sites

Option 3Visitor center only (no ticket)

The visitor center, grounds, and museums are free without a ticket. A good fallback that needs about 1.5–2 hours and no advance booking.
